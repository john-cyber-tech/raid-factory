# Interview notes — Priya Nair, Project Manager (Northway Consulting)
Date: 30 Sep. 

- Updates her own items after the weekly call. Needs to see only what's assigned to her, quick.
- Frustrated when items go "stale": risk raised in August, nobody has touched it since. Wants a nudge when nothing has changed in 2 weeks.
- Wants overdue items to be obvious, not buried.
- Closing items: she needs a status (Open / Mitigating / Closed) and a closed date, with a short reason. Never deletes things — "audit trail, the client's PMO checks."
- Issues vs risks confusion: team members call everything a risk. A tool that suggests whether it's a risk (might happen) or an issue (has happened) would help, but she'd correct it herself.
- Does not want email notifications. "I get enough email."
- Would use it only if it takes under 5 minutes a week of extra effort.
