# Interview notes — Sean Murphy, Delivery Manager (Northway Consulting)
Date: 29 Sep. Interviewer: Andrew. Raw notes, not cleaned.

- Runs 3 client engagements at once. "My RAID log is an Excel sheet per project and it's always out of date by Wednesday."
- Biggest pain: actions and risks come up *in* calls, and he only writes them down afterwards from memory. Misses about one in five.
- Wants to paste his call notes in and get a list of candidate items. "But I'm not letting a tool add things on its own. If it puts rubbish in the log the client sees it and I look daft."
- Items without an owner are the worst. Clients ask "who is doing this?" and he has no answer.
- Severity: Uses High/Medium/Low only. "Don't give me a 5x5 matrix, nobody reads it."
- Duplicates are constant: the same risk gets raised in three different calls and ends up three times in the sheet.
- Every Friday he sends the sponsor a one-page RAID summary. Takes him about 45 minutes. Wants it generated: top risks, new this week, overdue, items needing a decision.
- Concerned about confidentiality: client notes contain names and sometimes commercially sensitive numbers. Won't send anything to a service the client hasn't approved.
- Would like to see where an item came from ("what did they actually say?") when the client challenges it.
- Doesn't want another system to log in to. Local files are fine.

# Follow-up (email, 30 Sep)
"One more thing. Assumptions need a 'how do we check this' note, otherwise they just sit there forever. And dependencies should say who we are waiting on."
